# About Manh Pham

Manh Pham (full name Phạm Hữu Mạnh, also written Pham Huu Manh or Manh Pham Huu) is a final-year Information Systems student at VNU University of Engineering and Technology (VNU-UET) in Hanoi, Vietnam. He is the owner of this portfolio site and the builder of Cazung, the chatbot answering questions here.

## Quick facts

- Born in 2005, a Pisces.
- Hometown: Thanh Oai, formerly part of Ha Tay province, now part of Hanoi, Vietnam.
- Studies Information Systems at VNU-UET; expected to graduate in December 2026.
- Works across software engineering and AI.
- Languages: Vietnamese (native) and English (IELTS Academic 6.5).

## What he does

Manh has a foundation in both software engineering and artificial intelligence. He worked as a Backend & Database Intern at Vietnam National Data Application JSC (NaDat), on the data and backend of a national-level science and technology portal.

Outside class he builds his own software end to end. Two examples are this portfolio platform, which has a retrieval-augmented chatbot, and Cazung, a desktop AI companion that runs on local language models. His undergraduate thesis is in multi-agent reinforcement learning: it builds a simulated economy in which AI agents learn tax policy.

In the long run he wants to go deep into AI research, or to grow into a technical leader and eventually a CTO.
